Coexisting with endpoint protection

        

          Forensic tooling has a real deployment problem: unsigned, unfamiliar
          collectors get quarantined by endpoint protection, and the analyst
          loses the evidence they came for. JOCKY's answer is not to hide from
          the security stack — it is to work with it. These are the constraints
          the runtime is built to, and they are what make its output
          defensible as evidence.
